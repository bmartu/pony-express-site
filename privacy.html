Pony Express Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Pony Express is a private, single-user application for personal purchase and shipment tracking.

Google account access

Pony Express requests read-only access to Gmail so that it can identify order confirmations, shipping notifications, carrier tracking numbers, and delivery information.

Pony Express does not send, delete, modify, archive, or label Gmail messages.

Data storage

Information needed for package tracking may be stored locally on systems controlled by the application owner. This may include order numbers, vendor names, carrier names, tracking numbers, shipment status, message identifiers, and selected message content used as evidence for shipment tracking.

Data sharing

Google user data obtained by Pony Express is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Use of Google data

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only for the package- and order-tracking functions described above. Pony Express is intended to comply with Google's applicable user-data and limited-use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the application owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
